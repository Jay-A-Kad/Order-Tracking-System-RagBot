# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty Coverage

Unless otherwise stated on a product's listing, items purchased come with a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date shown in your order history. This warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship that exist at the time of delivery and become apparent under normal use.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty does not cover:
- Damage caused by accident, misuse, or improper handling after delivery
- Normal wear and tear from regular use
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect the item's function

## Filing a Warranty Claim

To file a warranty claim, provide your order ID and a description of the defect. Warranty claims can be filed at any point within the coverage period, regardless of whether the standard 30-day return window has passed, since warranty coverage and the returns policy are separate and operate on different timelines.

## Warranty Remedies

Depending on the nature of the defect and product availability, a warranty claim may be resolved through repair, replacement with the same or a comparable item, or a refund. Which remedy applies is determined after the claim is reviewed, and is not guaranteed to be the customer's first choice in every case.

## Extended Warranties

Some product categories may offer an optional extended warranty at checkout, which extends coverage beyond the standard 1-year period. If purchased, the extended warranty terms and duration are confirmed in the order confirmation for that item.

## Relationship to Returns and Refunds

A warranty claim is different from a standard return. A return is for an item you no longer want, within 30 days of delivery, regardless of whether it has a defect. A warranty claim is specifically for a manufacturing defect discovered after the return window has typically closed, and follows the warranty timeline instead of the returns timeline described in the returns policy.
