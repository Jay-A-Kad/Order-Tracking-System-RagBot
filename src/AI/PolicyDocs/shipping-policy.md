# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1 business day of being placed. Once an order moves from "Placed" to "Processing" status, our fulfillment team prepares the items for shipment. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays begin processing on the next business day.

## Shipping Methods and Carriers

We ship using a rotating set of carriers, including USPS, UPS, FedEx, and DHL, depending on package size, destination, and carrier availability at the time of shipment. You cannot request a specific carrier for a standard order. Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number and an estimated delivery date, which you can also check anytime by asking about your order status.

## Estimated Delivery Times

Standard delivery typically takes 2 to 6 business days from the day an order ships, not from the day it was placed. Estimated delivery dates shown at checkout and in order tracking are estimates based on carrier averages, not guarantees. Delivery times may be longer during high-volume periods such as major holidays, or due to weather and carrier delays outside of our control.

## Shipping Costs

Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on order weight, dimensions, and destination. We periodically run free-shipping promotions on orders above a minimum order value; any such promotion will be clearly displayed at checkout.

## Order Status After Shipment

Once an order ships, it will pass through the following statuses in order: Shipped, then Out for Delivery, then Delivered. Each status change is timestamped and visible in your order history. If your order shows "Shipped" but the tracking number appears inactive for more than 2 business days, please contact support, as this can indicate a carrier scanning delay rather than a lost package.

## Shipping Restrictions

We currently ship only within the regions supported by our checkout system. We do not ship to P.O. boxes for items requiring a signature on delivery. If an item in your cart cannot be shipped to your address, this will be flagged before you complete checkout.

## Lost or Damaged Shipments

If a package is marked "Delivered" but you have not received it, please wait 24 hours, as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered slightly before physical drop-off. If it still has not arrived after 24 hours, or if a package arrives visibly damaged, contact support with your order ID so we can open a carrier investigation or arrange a replacement.
