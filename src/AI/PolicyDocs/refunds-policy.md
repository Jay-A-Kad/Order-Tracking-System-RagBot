# Refunds Policy

## When Refunds Are Issued

A refund is issued once a returned item has been received and inspected, or immediately in cases where an order is cancelled before it ships. Refunds are not issued at the moment a return is approved or a return label is generated — only after the returned item physically arrives back and passes inspection.

## Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. We do not issue refunds to a different payment method, store credit, or a third-party account, except where required by law or explicitly offered as an alternative at the time of return approval.

## Refund Timing

Once a returned item is received and inspected, refunds are typically processed within 3 to 5 business days. After the refund is processed on our end, it may take an additional 2 to 10 business days for the funds to appear on your original payment method or statement, depending on your bank or card issuer. This second delay is outside of our control.

## Order Cancellations Before Shipment

If an order is still in "Placed" or "Processing" status, it can be cancelled before it ships, and a full refund is issued immediately upon cancellation, since no item has been shipped yet. Once an order reaches "Shipped" status, it can no longer be cancelled outright and must instead go through the standard return process after delivery.

## Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be issued instead of a full refund in specific situations, such as when a returned item shows signs of use beyond normal inspection, or when only part of a multi-item order is returned. The refunded amount in these cases reflects only the eligible portion of the original order.

## Refunds for Damaged or Incorrect Items

If an item arrived damaged or the wrong item was shipped, the full order amount for that item, including any shipping charges paid, is refunded once the issue is confirmed. In many cases, a replacement can be sent instead of a refund if the customer prefers to still receive the item.

## Checking Refund Status

Refund status is not shown directly in order status history in the same way shipping status is. If you believe a refund is overdue based on the timelines above, contact support with your order ID so the payment can be traced.
